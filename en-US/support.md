# LOWGRID – Help & Support

[Deutsch](../de-DE/support.md)

## Get in touch

For game questions, bug reports and suggestions, visit [LOWGRID support on GitHub](https://github.com/LilaQ/lowgrid-ios-issues/issues). You need a GitHub account to post a request. Existing answers are readable without signing in.

Requests are public. Describe the problem in words and leave out personal information, passwords, payment receipts and private player names. Do not upload files, screenshots or diagnostic logs.

Useful details include the game mode, the steps that led to the problem and whether it happens again after restarting. Include only information you are comfortable sharing publicly.

## Getting started

Choose **New Game** in the main menu to pick a mode. **Training** walks you through revealing, drawing, swapping and clearing matching columns at the table. Help is also available during a game.

For a shared game, choose the mode for playing on one device. Two to eight people can play. Pass the device when the handoff screen appears; the next person confirms when they are ready.

After drawing, you can swap the card with one of your own. To discard a card drawn from the deck, tap it again, then reveal one of your hidden cards. A card taken from the face-up discard pile must be swapped into your grid.

## Continuing a game

LOWGRID saves the local game on your device. Choose **Continue Game** in the main menu to resume. Before a new game or daily challenge replaces an unfinished game, LOWGRID asks you to confirm. **Cancel** keeps your save; **Replace Game** starts over.

Training has its own save and does not replace your regular game. To continue an unfinished lesson, choose **New Game → Training**. **Continue Game** gives priority to your regular game.

Games are not synchronized between devices. Deleting the app can remove local games, names and settings.

## Sound, motion and readability

Settings let you adjust music and effects separately, switch haptics on or off, reduce motion and change text size. LOWGRID also respects the system Reduce Motion setting.

## Purchases

Apple handles purchases. If you have a purchase problem, check that you are using the same Apple Account as for the original purchase, then restart LOWGRID with an internet connection. Do not share receipts or payment details in public support requests.

For information about requesting a refund, visit [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223).
