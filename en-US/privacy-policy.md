# LOWGRID – Privacy Policy

[Deutsch](../de-DE/privacy-policy.md)

Updated September 8, 2026

## Contact

For privacy questions and requests about your data, contact LOWGRID at [info@emudev.de](mailto:info@emudev.de). For help with the game, visit the [support page](support.md).

## Playing locally

Local games do not require a LOWGRID account. Player names, saved games, training progress, preferences and the most recent 100 completed matches are stored on your device. LOWGRID does not send this local game data to its own server or synchronize it between devices.

This information lets you continue games and retain your preferences. It remains until replaced or removed. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading the app keeps that data. Device backups may contain copies, which you can manage through Apple's backup settings.

Local play does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location. There is no additional analytics service for local gameplay.

## Purchases through Apple

Apple processes available in-app purchases. LOWGRID uses Apple's StoreKit to check purchase status, unlock Premium and disable advertising. LOWGRID does not receive payment details such as credit card numbers. Deleting the app does not delete Apple's purchase records. Apple's processing is covered by the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/).

## Advertising and privacy choices

Where advertising is available, LOWGRID uses Google AdMob and Google's consent form. Ad requests start only when its current privacy check permits them. Required privacy options are accessible in Settings. Verified Premium access disables ad requests.

Google may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, device identifiers, ad interactions, crash reports and performance data for advertising, measurement and diagnostics. LOWGRID currently does not request system tracking permission. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing. See [Google Privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en) and [Google's ad SDK disclosures](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure).

LOWGRID also stores ad impression times locally to limit frequency. Google's consent system manages privacy choices. Google may process information outside your country; its privacy notices and available consent information explain recipients, retention and safeguards.

## Support and external pages

When you send an email, your sender details and the information you provide are processed to respond to your request. Share only what is needed. You can use the same contact to request access, correction or deletion.

When you open an external link, the provider receives connection information needed to serve that page. GitHub hosts these documents; the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) also applies when you visit them. Reading them does not require an account.

## Your choices

You can remove local data by deleting the app and change or withdraw required advertising consent through the privacy options. Withdrawal applies going forward. Use Apple's and Google's privacy services to manage information they already process.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or transfer your data, object to processing, and complain to a competent privacy authority. For requests concerning LOWGRID, contact [info@emudev.de](mailto:info@emudev.de).

These notices will be updated when features or data handling change.
